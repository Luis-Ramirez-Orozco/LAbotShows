LA Bot Shows: LED Robot Performers for Events in the Los Angeles Area

LA Bot Shows provides LED robot performers for events in the Los Angeles area. The illuminated robots dance, interact with guests and pose for photos.

Events

Weddings, quinceañeras, sweet 16s, corporate events, birthdays, concerts and all other types of events.

The show

45 minutes in total: 30 minutes of dancing and 15 minutes of photos.

Pricing

Monday to Thursday: $450. Friday to Sunday: $499. Add-ons: CO2 cannon $100, 50 balloons $25, 25 LED noodles $25, money shooter $25, extra robot $300 (each).

How to book

Use Book Now to pick a date and send a request by email to labotshows@gmail.com or by text to 949-395-0250. Requests are answered within 24 hours.
